# Welcome to CriticAI 👋

**CriticAI is a community of practice to critically enquiry about AI’s adoption in Higher Education**

## Motivation:

CriticAI arises from the recognition that how AI is being adopted and implemented in Academia/HE is problematic and, consequently, a matter of concern to many individuals. Yet expressing concern or disagreement is often challenging, isolating and disempowering for those who want to do it.

There's a need to create (safe) spaces and opportunities to talk with like-minded people.

There's a need to learn how to manage emotional processes.

There's a need to make our voices heard.

There's a need to challenge defeatism.

There's a need to shape a better present and future of AI usage in Academia/HE.

CriticAI aspires to address these needs through collective action informed by **respect, care and pragmatism**.
