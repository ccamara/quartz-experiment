> Our AI Collective is working to strengthen civic power in the age of AI. Through education, research, and public participation, we aim to help communities build understanding, reject the myth that an AI future is inevitable, challenge profit-driven systems, and reimagine technology in service of justice, collective wellbeing and public benefit.
